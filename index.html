Research principles

History, not mythology presented as fact

The four supplied research reports are the foundation of this website. They distinguish surviving evidence, scholarly interpretation and later tradition. That distinction is kept throughout the reading experience.

Documented evidence

Objects, inscriptions, dated texts, museum records, photographs and codified sporting records.

Interpretation & legend

Stories, possible connections and origin traditions are identified as such where the reports caution against treating them as proven history.

The four games do not all have the same kind of relationship to their modern counterparts. In particular, the supplied Gilli-Danda report does not establish direct descent into cricket; and the wrestling report describes continuity through change rather than one unbroken, uniform ruleset.
